## AirTake · Moblin edition — Windows x64 preview

Run **AirTake.exe** from the ZIP. On first launch click **Подготовить движок** to download the verified FFmpeg build. Select Fifine, a recording folder and your LAN address, then import the Moblin QR on iPhone.

No custom iPhone app or signing. Existing Moblin app only. Original video stream copy, local microphone WAV, automatic or manual audio alignment, MKV/MP4 export, reconnect parts, disk guard and diagnostics.

**Hardware qualification is pending:** Moblin 4K/120 is experimental. Synthetic encrypted SRT and audio/export tests do not guarantee sustained 4K/120 on a real iPhone/Wi-Fi. Source-side loss and missing intervals cannot be recovered. Stop AirTake before stopping Moblin.

This is the independent Moblin edition; it does not replace the repository's concurrent native iOS implementation. FFmpeg is downloaded directly from its vendor, not bundled. The executable is unsigned.
